About me

Har kommer du hitta mina färdigheter och verktyg jag kan. Samt lite mer om min bakgrund.

Futuregames & Projects

During my studies at Futuregames, I have worked on several projects within UX, UI and Experience Design. I have worked with user research, prototyping, user testing, concept development and visual design. Many of the projects have been team-based, where I have often focused on research, idea development and the visual side of the projects.

Tools

Figma I use Figma for wireframes, UI design, prototyping and developing visual concepts. Miro I use Miro for brainstorming, research, workshops and structuring ideas together with others. FigJam I use FigJam to quickly collect ideas, create mind maps and develop concepts together as a team.

Other Tools & Free Time

Photoshop I use Photoshop for sketching, drawing and concept art. Illustrator I use Illustrator to create simple logos, posters and other graphic elements. Blender I use Blender to create 3D models and scenes that I can use as references for my drawings. I am also currently learning more about concept art and developing my skills in digital visual creation.

Outside of School

When I'm not studying, I enjoy creating things in my free time as well. I paint, sketch and experiment with different creative projects. I also enjoy snowboarding, volleyball, anime and music. I simply like having projects I can get lost in — whether it's designing something digitally or creating something with my hands.
